# General Plan for Determining the Viability Of lms6dsop (IMU)

## To be Purchased
The following components need to be purchased.
- [Adapter Board - $16.20](https://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/steval-mki229a.html)
- [Evaluation Board - $106.00](https://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/steval-mki109d.html)

The prices above are direct from the manufacturer, it may be cheaper and/or faster to buy from a third party like digikey once shipping and lead time are taken into account.

## Workflow
The adapter board is a small PCB with the same sensor as the lms6dsop, and the evaluation board provides power and handles interfacing between the sensor and a computer (via USB).
This will allow us to quickly determine the viability of the lms6dsop for positioning purposes without having to design a custom circuits for interperting the sensor output and providing power. The manufacturer provides the necessary software and testing the viability of the IMU should not be difficult once the boards are in hand.
